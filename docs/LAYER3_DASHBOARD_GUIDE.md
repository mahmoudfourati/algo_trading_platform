## 📊 Dashboard Sections

### Section 1: Overview

**Panels:**
- **Service Health** - Shows if Layer 3 is running (1 = healthy, 0 = down)
- **Tick Consumption Rate** - Real-time rate of scored ticks being processed
- **Cumulative Counters** - Total ticks processed and signals published

**What to look for:**
- ✅ Service Health = 1
- ✅ Tick rate > 0 (typically 20-30 ticks/sec)
- ⚠️ Signals Published = 0 is normal (only LONG/SHORT signals are published, not HOLD)

---

### Section 2: Technical Indicators

#### RSI Indicator
**Range**: 0-100  
**Interpretation**:
- **< 30**: Oversold (potential LONG signal)
- **30-35**: Approaching oversold
- **35-65**: Neutral zone
- **65-70**: Approaching overbought
- **> 70**: Overbought (potential SHORT signal)

**Current Strategy Thresholds**:
- LONG trigger: RSI < 35 (NORMAL mode) or < 30 (CONSERVATIVE mode)
- SHORT trigger: RSI > 65 (NORMAL mode) or > 70 (CONSERVATIVE mode)

#### MACD Histogram
**Interpretation**:
- **Positive & Increasing**: Bullish momentum
- **Positive & Decreasing**: Weakening bullish momentum
- **Negative & Decreasing**: Bearish momentum
- **Negative & Increasing**: Weakening bearish momentum
- **Crossing zero**: Potential trend change

**Strategy Requirement**:
- LONG: Histogram must be positive and increasing
- SHORT: Histogram must be negative and decreasing

#### Bollinger Band Width
**Interpretation**:
- **Low width (< 0.01)**: Low volatility, tight range
- **Medium width (0.01-0.03)**: Normal volatility
- **High width (> 0.03)**: High volatility, wide range

**Strategy Requirement**:
- LONG: Price must be at or below lower Bollinger Band
- SHORT: Price must be at or above upper Bollinger Band

#### Order Flow Imbalance (OFI)
**Range**: -1 to +1  
**Interpretation**:
- **> 0.1**: Strong buy pressure (bullish)
- **-0.1 to 0.1**: Neutral
- **< -0.1**: Strong sell pressure (bearish)

**Strategy Requirement**:
- LONG: OFI must be > 0.1
- SHORT: OFI must be < -0.1

---

### Section 3: Signal Generation

#### Signal Direction Count
Shows cumulative count of signals generated by direction:
- **HOLD**: No actionable signal (most common)
- **LONG**: Buy signal
- **SHORT**: Sell signal

**Note**: Only LONG and SHORT signals are published to Kafka. HOLD signals are tracked but not published.

#### EMA Crossover Events
Tracks when fast EMA crosses slow EMA:
- **Bullish Cross**: Fast EMA crosses above slow EMA (potential uptrend)
- **Bearish Cross**: Fast EMA crosses below slow EMA (potential downtrend)

#### Total Signals Published
Shows how many LONG/SHORT signals have been sent to Kafka for Layer 4 (Risk Management).

**Expected**: This will be 0 most of the time because the strategy is conservative and requires ALL conditions to be met.

#### Last Signal Size %
Shows the position size of the last published signal (if any).

### Section 4: Current Indicator Values (Gauges)

- **Current MACD Histogram (5m)**: Live MACD with bullish/bearish zones

## 🎯 Understanding Why No Signals Are Generated

The Layer 3 strategy requires **ALL** of the following conditions to be met for a signal:

### For LONG Signal:
1. ✅ RSI < 35 (or < 30 in CONSERVATIVE mode)
2. ✅ MACD histogram positive and increasing
3. ✅ Price at or below lower Bollinger Band
4. ✅ EMA alignment (recent crossover or EMAs close together)
5. ✅ OFI > 0.1 (buy pressure)
6. ✅ Higher timeframe (1h) confluence (at least 2 of 3 conditions)

### For SHORT Signal:
1. ✅ RSI > 65 (or > 70 in CONSERVATIVE mode)
2. ✅ MACD histogram negative and decreasing
3. ✅ Price at or above upper Bollinger Band
4. ✅ EMA alignment (recent crossover or EMAs close together)
5. ✅ OFI < -0.1 (sell pressure)
6. ✅ Higher timeframe (1h) confluence (at least 2 of 3 conditions)

**If ANY condition fails, the signal is HOLD.**

- **MACD**: Negative for BTC, near zero for ETH (weak momentum)

## 🔍 Monitoring Tips

### Healthy System Indicators:
- ✅ Service Health = 1
- ✅ Tick consumption rate > 0
- ✅ RSI, MACD, and BB Width updating in real-time
- ✅ Signal direction count increasing (even if all HOLD)

### Warning Signs:
- ⚠️ Service Health = 0 (service crashed)
- ⚠️ Tick consumption rate = 0 (not receiving data)
- ⚠️ Indicators not updating (data flow issue)
- ⚠️ Signal direction count not increasing (processing stalled)

### When to Expect Actual Trading Signals:
- **Market extremes**: RSI drops below 30 or rises above 70
- **Strong momentum**: MACD shows clear trending direction
- **Volatility expansion**: Price touches Bollinger Bands
- **Order flow confirmation**: OFI shows strong directional pressure
- **Timeframe alignment**: Both 5m and 1h indicators agree

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### Dashboard shows "No data"
1. Check time range (use "Last 15 minutes" or "Last 5 minutes")
2. Verify Layer 3 is running: `docker compose ps layer3-strategy`
3. Check Prometheus is scraping: http://localhost:9090/targets
4. Verify metrics are exported: http://localhost:9104/metrics

### Indicators not updating
1. Check Layer 3 logs: `docker compose logs layer3-strategy --tail 50`
2. Verify Layer 2 is publishing scored ticks
3. Check Kafka connectivity

### No signals being published
**This is normal!** The strategy is conservative and only publishes actionable LONG/SHORT signals. In neutral market conditions, all signals will be HOLD (which are not published to Kafka).

## 🎨 Dashboard Customization

### Adjusting Time Range
- Default: Last 15 minutes

All Layer 3 metrics are available in Prometheus:

- `strategy_indicator_*` - Technical indicators
- `strategy_signal_*` - Signal generation metrics

- Default: 5 seconds
